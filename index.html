What personal data do we process?

  • This site has no contact form, accounts, cookies or analytics/tracking tools; it does not collect information from you directly.
  • When you visit the site, GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages), which hosts it, keeps technical access logs such as your IP address, browser information, the page requested and the time of access.
  • If you email or call us, the information you send us, such as your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message.

Purposes and legal grounds
Access logs are processed to serve the site securely and reliably; contact details are processed to answer your request and correspond with you. This processing relies on legitimate interest under Article 5(2)(f) KVKK and, where your request concerns entering into a contract, on Article 5(2)(c).

How data is collected
Data is collected electronically: automatically by the hosting infrastructure when you visit the site, and from you when you contact us by email or phone.

Transfers
Access logs are processed by GitHub, Inc., which hosts the site, and email correspondence by Google, which provides our email service. As these providers' servers are located outside Türkiye, your personal data is transferred abroad; such transfers are made within the conditions of Article 9 KVKK. Your personal data is not shared with anyone else unless required by law.

Your rights
Under Article 11 KVKK you may learn whether your personal data is processed and request information about it, learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly, know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred, request its correction if incomplete or inaccurate, request its erasure or destruction under the conditions of KVKK and the notification of these actions to such third parties, object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

You can send requests in writing to the address above or to info@algorismu.com, in line with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller. Requests are concluded free of charge within 30 days at the latest.
